Bingo

Paste your words or phrases. You get a teacher link and a student link. Each student gets their own random board.

0 items. Use 9 to 100, up to 40 characters each.

How to type the list

One per line: no quotation marks needed, commas are fine inside a line.

apple
Hello, how are you?
I'd like a coffee

All on one line, separated by commas: put quotation marks around any item that contains a comma.

apple, banana, "Hello, how are you?", coffee

Repeated items are removed. Extra spaces are tidied.

The session lasts 24 hours, then is deleted. Student names are never collected.
